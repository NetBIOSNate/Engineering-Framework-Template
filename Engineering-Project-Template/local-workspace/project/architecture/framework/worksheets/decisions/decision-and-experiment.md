# Worksheet for a decision and its experiment

Give each record a stable ID. Write down the question before a favored design becomes a commitment. Replace the prompts below and retain older accepted records when a later decision supersedes them.

| Field | Project entry |
| --- | --- |
| ID, title and status | [Proposed, accepted, superseded or retired, with date] |
| Decider and consulted owners | [Person or role with authority, affected parties and dissent if relevant] |
| Decision boundary | [System, supported configurations, deadline and cost of delay] |
| Drivers and hard limits | [Scenario or requirement IDs, limits and units] |
| Evidence and uncertainty | [What is observed, calculated, assumed or unknown, and under which conditions] |

**Decision and rationale.** [Chosen option or bounded trial, why the balance favors it, and the evidence that could reverse it.]

**Consequences and ownership.** [Changed views and contracts, costs, risk owner, remaining obligations, and migration or exit steps.]

## Experiment card

- Prediction and competing explanation — [what should happen and what else could explain the observation].
- Setup — [configuration, data or test fixture, load, mode and independent observation point].
- Disturbance — [what changes, when it occurs and which boundary it exercises].
- Acceptance — [measure, unit, population, tolerance and threshold agreed before the run].
- Results — [planned or observed, evidence reference, limitations and owner].
- Follow-up — [decision date, trigger, reviewer and successor ID if superseded].

**Closure check.** Someone outside the discussion should be able to name the best rejected option, the accepted downside and the condition under which the decision returns for review.
