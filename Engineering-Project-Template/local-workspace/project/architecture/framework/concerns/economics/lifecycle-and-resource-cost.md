# Compare whole-life cost and constrained resources

Evaluate spend against a unit of useful outcome and a declared planning horizon. A quoted unit price misses integration, delayed delivery, operation, assurance, migration, disposal and the ongoing cost of carrying an extra architectural boundary. State whose budget pays and which costs the design transfers to another team or user.

## Construct the model

| Cost class | Typical inputs |
| --- | --- |
| One-time | Design, integration, qualification, migration, fixtures, training and initial tooling |
| Fixed recurring | Support contract, minimum fleet, baseline capacity, rotation staffing and spare inventory |
| Variable | Work per transaction, transported bytes, energy, replacement cycles and human intervention |
| Exceptional or terminal | Recovery exercises, loss scenarios, data extraction, field recalls and retirement |
| Constraints outside price | Lead time, finite energy, scarce skills, service access and irreversible commitments |

Use the same mission volume, service target, time period and accounting boundary for every candidate. Show assumptions as ranges and identify which variables move together. Account for capital timing or discounting with the owner of the economic model when it materially changes the decision.

## Example of a break-even point

For an illustrative year, option A costs 40,000 fixed units plus 0.04 per completed outcome. Option B costs 16,000 plus 0.10 per outcome. Equating totals gives 400,000 annual outcomes. At 200,000 the totals are 48,000 and 36,000. At 600,000 they are 64,000 and 76,000. The model assumes equivalent service, one-year fixed costs and variable cost on successful outcomes. Include retries, idle reserve and labor if they differ. A cheaper candidate that misses a hard requirement leaves the comparison.

For energy-sensitive systems, measure energy per useful outcome, baseline idle demand, peak power and end-of-life battery capacity. For a staffed service, include time to diagnose, repair and train. Test the dominant assumption and attach the result to the [decision record](../../worksheets/decisions/decision-and-experiment.md). Record the demand or price threshold that should reopen the choice.
