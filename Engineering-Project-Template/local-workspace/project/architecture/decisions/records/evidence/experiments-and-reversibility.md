# Choose evidence proportionate to the decision

Start with the prediction whose failure would reverse a choice. Decide what counts as disconfirming evidence before running the experiment. A benchmark that measures the wrong bottleneck can make a costly decision look settled.

| Uncertainty | Cheap first evidence | Stronger evidence when consequences justify it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer tolerates a new representation | Read actual old fixtures with the proposed reader | Mixed-version exercise using real consumer implementations |
| A dependency can recover inside the budget | Bound each serial delay and inspect critical assumptions | Cold-start or restore under representative load and access conditions |
| A physical replacement fits in service | Review tolerances, datums and access envelope | Fit and service trial with production-like parts and tools |
| Users can recognize an ambiguous outcome | Walk the scenario with a domain expert | Observe representative operators completing the task under realistic conditions |

## Design an experiment

Specify the configuration, inputs, disturbance, observation points, measures and threshold. Choose an independent way to observe the result. A successful API response cannot establish that the downstream physical effect occurred. Record negative controls, such as a deliberately duplicated identity or disabled protection, when they can safely demonstrate that the check detects the fault it claims to detect.

List known differences between the experiment and the intended environment. A fast storage simulator can establish protocol behavior while leaving physical endurance unknown. State the inference each result permits and the exposure left open. Budget time, materials and operator access in proportion to the reversal cost.

Classify evidence as proposed, calculated, observed or accepted by a named authority. Keep failed trials and configuration changes associated with the result. A reversible trial needs a rollback owner and a rule for isolating its effects. An irreversible trial needs a representative substitute or a separately authorized operating envelope. Finish when the decision owner can choose, commission one specific follow-up, or defer with a bounded consequence.
