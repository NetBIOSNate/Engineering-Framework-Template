# Evaluate abuse, disclosure and privacy

Select one asset and one unwanted outcome. Work backward from the consequence to the authority, data and physical access that could produce it. A threat can come from a legitimate insider making a mistake, a compromised device, a malicious client or a dependency returning plausible but wrong information.

## Walk the attack or misuse path

1. Draw the entry point and subsequent trust crossings. Mark who can manipulate each input, including clocks, labels, restore images, support exports and update packages.
2. Specify the consequence, prerequisites, existing protection, maximum exposure, detection signal and recovery owner. Challenge controls that share a root credential, management server or physical enclosure.
3. Trace each sensitive field to a purpose, permitted readers, retention rule, derivative and disposal route. Use project-specific classification and obligations agreed with their owners.
4. Select a design change or a verifiable claim. Restrict collection, minimize exported detail, enforce access at the resource, split authority or isolate the high-impact action.

## A risky diagnostic export

An operator needs the event ID, device model, failure category and time bucket to diagnose delayed upload. A raw export also contains precise location and an authentication header. Define a diagnostic contract that emits the minimum fields and give site-specific access an expiry. Consider whether a rare device model and a small time bucket can still identify a person. Assign a reviewer who understands that population.

Test with a synthetic credential-shaped value and a synthetic person's identifier through logs, errors, cache, exports and restore. Demonstrate the approved roles can investigate the delay and a second site's support account cannot retrieve the record. Record the scope and residual inference risk. When a deletion or restriction must persist after restore, identify the separately retained instruction and exercise reapplication before serving restored data.

An assurance argument should say which misuse path was exercised, under which configuration, and which claims still depend on policy or people. Attach those results to the [scenario review](../../../framework/worksheets/assurance/scenario-and-review.md) and the affected [authority boundary](../identity-and-authority.md).
