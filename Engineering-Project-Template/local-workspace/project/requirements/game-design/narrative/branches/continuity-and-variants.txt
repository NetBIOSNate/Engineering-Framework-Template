Specify continuity across branching stories

Reachable story states should preserve the meaning of prior choices across save, resume, localization and content changes.

1. Identify each decision with enduring consequences. Give it a stable fact or state identity and define who can change it. Separate the recorded choice from localized dialogue, animation and other ways it is presented.
2. Sketch a branch-and-merge graph. At each merge, list facts the next scene assumes. If either witness can provide a clue, test both paths and the path where neither is available. Decide whether the latter blocks progress, triggers an alternate scene or signals an authoring error.
3. Trace interruption after recording a choice and before displaying its consequence. Agree how the participant recovers the context and how repeated loading treats one-time effects. Include alternative presentation for clues that depend on color, sound or timed input.
4. For a content revision, enumerate saved states entering a removed branch. Define a compatible continuation, deliberate migration or supported endpoint. Record who approves any changed meaning.

Review output. A reachability table links choice, required facts, allowed successor and evidence for a complete path in each supported variant. The method also applies to guided training and procedural simulations.
