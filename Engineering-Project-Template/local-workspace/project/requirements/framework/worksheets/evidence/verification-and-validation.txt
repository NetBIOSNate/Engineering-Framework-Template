Worksheet | Evidence and acceptance decision

Claim and revision                  [Requirement or stakeholder need to assess]
Case ID, author, reviewer           [Identity and roles]
Question                            [Conformance to a requirement or fitness for a use]
Method and independent oracle       [How measured and where the expected answer came from]
Applicable configuration            [Hardware, software, procedure, site, data and conditions]
Setup, population and sample basis  [Fixtures, sampling rationale, representativeness]
Intervention and observation points [Preconditions, stimulus, measurement start/end]
Limit and uncertainty rule          [Pass, fail and inconclusive regions agreed in advance]
Expected boundary cases             [Exact limit, outside limit, missing or contradictory input]
Actual observations                 [Evidence ID, time, values, units, missing observations]
Coverage gaps                       [Untested conditions and effect on generalization]
Disposition and reviewer            [Pass/fail/inconclusive, reason and date]
Operational validation              [User, task, intended setting, outcome, unresolved burden]
Acceptance decision                 [Scope authorized, exceptions, authority, expiry/trigger]

Have a second reviewer reconstruct the verdict using the recorded observations and rule. If the evidence is inconclusive, choose the next observation, a narrower scope or an explicit residual-risk decision. Test success, stakeholder validation and acceptance can occur at different times. Preserve each date and authority.
