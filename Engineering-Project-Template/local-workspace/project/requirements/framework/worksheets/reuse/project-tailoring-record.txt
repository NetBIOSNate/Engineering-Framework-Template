Example entry for “activities omitted or combined”
A single-team proof of concept combines mode and interface reviews in one annotated sequence. The systems lead will separate the supplier contract when an external company takes control of either endpoint. The current review assumes simulated users and an isolated environment.

Try the proposed agreement on a degraded scenario. Walk from the stakeholder loss to the person allowed to accept the residual risk. If the owner or the evidence is unclear, add the missing field or view before the project commits.
