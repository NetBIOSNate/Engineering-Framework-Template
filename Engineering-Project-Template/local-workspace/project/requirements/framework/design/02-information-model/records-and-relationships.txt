Design the information model

Choose stable identifiers that survive reorganizations. Store the current title, owner and status as editable properties. A drawing number, a supplier part or a folder name can change while the obligation persists. Give revised baselines distinct identities so a reviewer can recover what a team agreed on a specific date.

A small record model

Need         A stakeholder outcome, source, operating context and consequence.
Requirement  An observable obligation, parent or derivation, measure and owner.
Constraint   An externally fixed limit, its authority, scope and release condition.
Allocation   The element, interface or human role that carries all or part of a requirement.
Decision     Alternatives, selected design, assumptions, consequences and revisit trigger.
Case         The method, conditions and oracle used to challenge a claim.
Evidence     An actual observation, configuration, date, uncertainty and disposition.
Baseline     A named set of record revisions and the authority that accepted them.

Define the link verbs. A need motivates one or more requirements. A decision responds to requirements and may derive new ones. Allocation assigns responsibility. A case checks a requirement. Evidence records the outcome of executing that case. Keep validation of the stakeholder outcome visible as a separate relationship. Many-to-many links are normal, especially for qualities shared across components.

Review in both directions. Start at a costly design decision and find the need or constraint it serves. Start at a failed case and find every affected configuration and acceptance owner. Ask why any approved requirement lacks a parent or any safety-significant assumption lacks an owner. A derived protection can cite the hazard analysis that justifies it. A legacy record with an unknown source gets a named investigator and a decision deadline.

A spreadsheet, database or text register can implement these relationships. Give one location authority over each record and put references in the other representations.
